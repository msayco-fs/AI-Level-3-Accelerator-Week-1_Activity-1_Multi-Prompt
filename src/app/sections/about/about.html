Our story

We opened because nobody would sell us a decent bowl of matcha

In 2019 we were two baristas and one very stubborn tea obsessive renting a narrow unit on Aurora Boulevard. The plan was small: source leaf we actually believed in, learn to whisk it properly, and refuse to sweeten our way out of a bad cup.

That is still the whole plan. The espresso programme came later, for the mornings when tea will not do, and the kitchen came after that because people kept asking what they could eat. Everything else we have left alone.
